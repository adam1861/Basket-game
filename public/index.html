Choose your controls to begin.

Your hands are the controllers.

Choose Hand tracking to control the paddles with your palms, or Finger tracking to control them with your index fingers. Move both hands up and down for a four-second calibration.

Using a keyboard? Move the left paddle with W / S and the right with ↑ / ↓. Space pauses. R restarts.

Build your rally.

Hit near a paddle’s edge for a sharper angle. Every return adds a point and a little speed. Miss three times and the run ends.
